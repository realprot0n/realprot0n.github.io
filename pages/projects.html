Classroom Music Player

I just recently made this one public (open-source); its meant to be a way for teachers to make collaborative playlists for their classroom. Teachers will be able to make a google form to get youtube links to songs from their students. Currently, this is barely even a mnimum viable product (MVP). Instructions to install and run this can be found in the GitHub repository.

Planned Features

  • Moderation section for, removing, and reordering videos
  • Way to add new videos later without having to redownload everything
  • Fixing/remidiating errors when downloading videos (time-out errors)
  • An actual audio player for it; with the generic controls you would expect, plus something showing the suggestor's name (if not anonymous), and the video's thumbnail.
